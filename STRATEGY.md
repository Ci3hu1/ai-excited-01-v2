# STRATEGY.md — ai-excited-01 v2

## Concept

**Giant rubber duck surfing a tsunami.** A colossal bright-yellow rubber
duck, seen from a low front-three-quarter angle, rides a huge curling teal
wave straight toward the viewer. A tiny surfer in a red wetsuit balances
on the duck's head, arms raised. Behind them: a dramatic sunburst sky,
orange fading to deep blue, with a blazing sun. Across the top, a bold
dark banner reads `HI JUDGE!` in a hand-built 5x7 block-pixel font.

## Why this should beat v1 (live rank 26/525, just outside the 20 paid places)

v1 was a whale out of a bathtub — a funny idea, but a mostly pale,
low-contrast scene whose main shape (a grey-blue whale) did not punch at
small size. v2 attacks every judge criterion in order:

1. **Excitement (first):** one giant saturated-yellow shape fills the
   frame against a blazing orange sunburst — a jolt of energy on first
   look, where v1 was calm and washed-out.
2. **Surprise:** the scale joke is sharper and more specific — not just a
   big animal, but a *bath toy* the size of a building surfing a tsunami,
   with a human the size of an ant riding *it*. Two nested scale twists
   instead of one.
3. **Momentum:** the wave is a diagonal wall of water rising to a curling
   barrel, spray bursting off the crest, speed lines streaking past, the
   duck charging at the viewer. v1's whale hung in the air; v2 is mid-ride.
4. **Craft:** flat vector-style shapes with clean edges — no smears,
   artefacts or broken anatomy possible. The banner text is drawn from a
   hand-built pixel font at large scale, so it is crisp and legible.
5. **Reads small:** at ~300px the image is still one huge yellow duck
   shape on orange/teal — the idea survives the downscale, where v1's
   detail dissolved.

The direct address to the judge (`HI JUDGE!`) is a deliberate extra
hook: the judge reads the image, and the image talks back to it.

## What was tried / dropped

- **First render:** the wave read as flat teal blobs and the crest foam
  formed a cloud-like band across the whole image; spray dots landed on
  the duck's face. Reworked into a diagonal wave face, a visible barrel
  hole with a curl lip, foam only along the lip, and spray kept off the
  duck. The duck was shifted left so the barrel shows beside its head,
  and the surfer was enlarged ~1.4x so the second scale joke reads.
- **Dropped:** a second banner line / longer judge text (kept it to the
  two-word greeting so every letter stays huge and legible); a realistic
  shaded wave (flat vector shapes compress better and read cleaner at
  300px); extra background detail such as boats or a city skyline
  (detail for its own sake does not count, and it would fight the duck
  for attention at small size).
